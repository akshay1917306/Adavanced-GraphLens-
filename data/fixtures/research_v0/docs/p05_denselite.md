# DenseLite: A Small Dense Retriever for Low-Cost Search

**Authors:** Jonah Pell
**Affiliation:** Coastline AI Lab
**Synthetic fixture document. All names, methods, datasets, and results are fictional.**

## Abstract

DenseLite is a compact dense retriever with 22 million parameters. It targets
open-domain passage retrieval on modest hardware and runs on a laptop CPU.

## Evaluation

DenseLite is evaluated on MiraQA (56.1 exact match when paired with a reader)
and on ChainBench (40.9 exact match).

## Notes

DenseLite is used as a building block by several later methods, including
HopFuse.
