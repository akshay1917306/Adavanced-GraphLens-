CommunityLens: Summarizing Research Themes with Graph Communities

Authors: Rhea Lindqvist, Priya Saldanha

Affiliation: Harborview University

Synthetic fixture document. All names, methods, datasets, and results are fictional.

Abstract

CommunityLens groups entities in a knowledge graph into communities and writes a short summary for each one. It targets corpus-level summarization, such as answering "what are the main themes in this collection".

Method

CommunityLens applies hierarchical Leiden clustering to an entity graph built from paper abstracts, then summarizes each community.

Evaluation

We evaluate on ThemeSet, a set of 120 corpus-level questions. Human judges prefer CommunityLens summaries over a vector RAG baseline in 68 percent of pairs.
