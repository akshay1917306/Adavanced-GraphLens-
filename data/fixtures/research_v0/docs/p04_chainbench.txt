ChainBench: Long Reasoning Chains Over Technical Reports

Authors: Ada Morrow, Jonah Pell
Affiliation: Coastline AI Lab
Synthetic fixture document. All names, methods, datasets, and results are fictional.

Abstract
ChainBench is a benchmark of 1,500 questions whose answers require three to
five reasoning hops across fictional technical reports. It targets multi-hop
question answering and also includes 200 unanswerable questions to test
refusal behaviour.

Data
Each question comes with the list of supporting passages. The unanswerable
questions look similar but have no supporting passage in the corpus.

Baselines
BM25 ranking reaches 38.2 exact match. DenseLite reaches 40.9 exact match.
